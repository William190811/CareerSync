# AI extraction pilot: first 20 HN posts

Verified on 26 October 2026 using Databricks ai_extract version 2.0 in precision mode.

The fixed sample is ordered by created_at_unix, then item_id. It contains 18 readable advertisements and 2 unavailable records. No records outside this sample were sent for extraction.

## Stored outputs

- Review view: workspace.tech_careers.hn_extraction_sample20
- Fixed source sample: workspace.tech_careers.hn_extraction_sample20_inputs
- Versioned inference history: workspace.tech_careers.hn_extraction_sample20_results

The review view chooses the latest accepted result per post. Earlier attempts remain in the history table. Reviewing this view does not invoke AI.

## Verification

All 20 source records were inspected. The automated checks compare remote policy, explicit countries, seniority labels, numeric salary bounds, currency and pay period with reviewed expectations. They also check selected explicitly named skills, exact evidence substrings, unique IDs, output shapes, and salary bounds. All these checks passed.

Initial results included inferred countries, assumed annual salary periods, paraphrased quotes, and omitted seniority. Precision mode with stronger instructions corrected these. Two posts then received targeted retries for a missed explicit US abbreviation and omitted stack technologies. Outputs were generated by Databricks AI, not manually filled in.

## Limits

This chronological January 2020 sample is small and is not representative of the full archive. Passing these checks does not establish full-dataset accuracy or complete skill recall. Countries are deliberately limited to explicitly named countries or unambiguous country abbreviations; cities and regions remain in location_text. Empty country arrays therefore do not mean the job has no geographic restriction. Salary periods are not assumed annual. Skills and seniority are post-level unions across advertised roles and must not be presented as requirements for every individual role. Onsite strongly preferred is retained verbatim as evidence and does not prove remote work is forbidden.

The full archive has not been extracted.
